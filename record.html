Step 1 · Before you start

This page records your own voice reading sentences, to train a free personal AI voice for Tinig. Recordings stay in this browser until you export them.

  • Use a quiet room. Turn off fans and aircon if you can. A phone headset mic or any USB mic is fine.
  • Keep the same distance from the mic and the same speaking style the whole time. Read naturally, like a narrator.
  • Aim for at least 30 minutes of audio (more is better, 60 minutes is great). You can stop and continue another day — progress is saved.
